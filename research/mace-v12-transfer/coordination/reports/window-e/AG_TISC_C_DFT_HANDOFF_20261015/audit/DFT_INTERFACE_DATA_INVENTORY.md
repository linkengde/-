## Matched interaction-energy and force anchor

The 135-atom full DFT result and both component DFT results use the same fixed optimized coordinates partitioned by source atom IDs, the same cell and PBC, and compatible PBE/PAW numerical settings. Therefore the following single-geometry difference-of-differences is well-defined for this archive:

| DFT convention | E_interface (eV) | E_Ag (eV) | E_TSC (eV) | E_interaction = E_interface − E_Ag − E_TSC (eV) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Native extrapolated total energy | −939.821159610571 | −44.714091632522 | −872.877542503438 | **−22.229525474612** |
| Force-consistent free energy, FD 0.10 eV | −941.236116683098 | −45.116114998658 | −874.741340407191 | **−21.378661277249** |

Keep the two DFT energy conventions separate. Force-consistent free energy is the energy target paired with the finite-width forces. This one frozen-cell interaction energy does not provide an equilibrium-distance curve, registry dependence or a general adhesion energy.

The already archived LAMMPS A/B/C run0 logs have the same optimized geometry, types, PBC, `metal` units and potential hashes. Their printed `PotEng` values are −45.215392 eV (Ag-only), −718.6532 eV (TSC-only) and −811.78797 eV (full), giving an approximate, rounded classical cross interaction of −47.919378 eV. The roughly −26.541 eV difference from the DFT free-energy interaction anchor is diagnostic only: the LAMMPS thermodynamic output is printed at limited precision and it is one geometry, not an adhesion curve or a production fit score. No raw absolute total energy across unrelated references is compared.

## Reuse decision

Keep the 135-atom matched component labels as one cross-force/energy anchor. Keep the six V19 acquisitions as already-used local checks, not as new validation. Keep the ten V19 grid/width controls as numerical controls, not structural diversity. Do not copy any of these into new role assignments: TRAIN/DEV/TEST remain subject to C's frozen data-role registry. There is no clean independent structural family with matched interface/component labels in the reviewed archives.
