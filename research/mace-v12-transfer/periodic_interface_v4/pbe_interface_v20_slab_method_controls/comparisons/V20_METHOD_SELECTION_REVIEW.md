# V20 slab numerical-method review

Review date: 2026-10-25

## Evidence reviewed

This review consolidates the four registered fixed-geometry V20 comparisons against the retained `6x6x1`, `0.10 eV` V19 AgSi slab baseline. The new controls share the same 52-atom geometry, PBE/500 eV plane-wave setup, PAW data, SCF thresholds and mixer, and xy dipole correction. The comparison scripts re-verified each archive and exact geometry identity.

The provisional limits are 2 meV/atom for native extrapolated energy and force-consistent free energy, 0.01 eV/Å for force-vector RMS, and 0.02 eV/Å for the marked Ag–Si pair-force projection. Table entries are absolute differences, except the pair projection which is already reported as an absolute value here.

| Change from baseline | Native energy (meV/atom) | Free energy (meV/atom) | Force-vector RMS (eV/Å) | Pair projection (eV/Å) | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `4x4x1`, sigma `0.10 eV` | 0.6717 | 0.5610 | 0.01071 | 0.01588 | Exceeds force-RMS limit only |
| `8x8x1`, sigma `0.10 eV` | 0.1854 | 0.1411 | 0.00280 | 0.00266 | Passes all provisional limits |
| `6x6x1`, sigma `0.05 eV` | 0.0364 | 5.6536 | 0.01516 | 0.02804 | Exceeds free-energy, force-RMS, and pair-projection limits |
| `6x6x1`, sigma `0.20 eV` | 0.5890 | 22.0606 | 0.03039 | 0.04895 | Exceeds free-energy, force-RMS, and pair-projection limits |

The native energy differences are within limits for all four comparisons. This alone does not establish method stability: free energy and forces are sensitive to the smearing width, and the `0.20 eV` result deviates more than `0.05 eV`. The lower `4x4x1` mesh narrowly exceeds the force-RMS limit; `8x8x1` remains within the provisional limits relative to `6x6x1`.

## Provisional method choice

Retain `6x6x1`, sigma `0.10 eV` as the current reference for this frozen AgSi slab. It is bracketed by a lower mesh that misses the force-RMS budget and a higher mesh that passes the pairwise checks; both alternative smearings miss three budgets. This is a practical choice under the registered checks, not proof of absolute k-point convergence or universal optimality. The controls remain numerical-method evidence only and are not training labels or independent validation data.

## Impact on the approved V19 split

The read-only source audit found sigma `0.10 eV` in all 36 approved V19 training rows and all 3 development rows. Training rows use Gamma sampling (`1x1x1`) with fully periodic cells; the development rows use `6x6x1` slab sampling and nonperiodic z. Thus smearing width is consistent across the split, while k-point sampling and periodicity remain different. The V20 slab controls support the development slab's `0.10 eV` width but do not resolve that training/development method gap. No dataset or label was changed.

The row-level source evidence is in [`approved_v19_smearing_consistency.json`](approved_v19_smearing_consistency.json), generated by [`audit_approved_v19_smearing.py`](audit_approved_v19_smearing.py). The earlier focused width analysis is in [`SMEARING_SENSITIVITY_REVIEW.md`](SMEARING_SENSITIVITY_REVIEW.md).

## Scope boundary and next action

All four calculations and their comparisons are complete and archived. No additional DFT, model training, inference, or dataset edits are part of this review. Addressing the remaining Gamma/periodic versus slab-method gap requires a separately assigned method/data plan; until then, keep the current V19 labels unchanged and preserve this V20 evidence as control-only.
