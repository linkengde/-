# Ag–W TTM Baseline v1: validation record

Date: 2026-11-24
Generated by `scripts/validate_agw_ttm_baseline_v1.py`.

## Selected parameter set

- **Ag `Ce(Te), G(Te)`:** candidate curves digitized from Lin et al. (2008), Fig. 2(c,d), archived with an explicit digitization report.
- **Ag `ke(Te,Tl)`:** room-anchored Lin et al. (2008) Drude closure, `429 * [Ce_Ag_Lin(Te)/Ce_Ag_Lin(300)] * [(300 + a*300^2)/(Tl + a*Te^2)] W m^-1 K^-1, with a=A/B`. The 300 K value is `429.0 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹`. The room anchor uses the user's cited Ag bulk total `429 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹` as an electronic-dominant proxy. `A/B=1.898e-05 K⁻¹` is inferred from the XTANT 300 K channel ratio; this is a provisional shape closure, not an Ag-specific measured `ke(Te,Tl)` surface.
- **W `Ce(Te), G(Te)`:** Lin et al. (2008), Fig. 7(c,d), candidate digitized tables.
- **W `ke(Te,Tl)`:** Daraszewicz et al. (2014), equation (6): `ke_W(Te,Tl)=130.5*[Ce_W_Lin(Te)/Ce_W_Lin(300)]*(300/Tl)^1.5 W m^-1 K^-1`. It gives `130.5 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹` at 300 K. The published exponent was fitted to equilibrium conductivity behavior over 300–2500 K; use beyond that interval is an extrapolation.
- **W low-temperature `G0` reference:** Daraszewicz's measured `1.4(3)×10¹⁷ W m⁻³ K⁻¹` and ab-initio `1.65×10¹⁷ W m⁻³ K⁻¹` remain validation anchors, not replacements for the Lin `G(Te)` curve.

## W low-temperature coupling check

At 300 K, the Lin digitization gives `G=2.124e+17 W m⁻³ K⁻¹`. This is `51.7%` above the experimental central value and `25.0%` above its upper 1σ bound. It is also `28.7%` above the paper's ab-initio anchor.

**The Lin curve does not reproduce the Daraszewicz experimental `G0` within the quoted 1σ range.** Lin's 300 K value is itself in the documented below-600 K extrapolation region. This baseline therefore retains a visible source discrepancy; the Lin curve was not rescaled to force agreement.

## Ag/W electronic interface assumption

The first Ag–W grid implementation uses a face-based Ag/W electron conductance of `1.0 GW m⁻² K⁻¹`. This provisional value lies between the Au/Pt and Au/Al method analogues reported by Li et al. (2024); it is not an Ag/W measurement. Transfers are equal and opposite between the phase electron grids. The mesh couples unlike-phase fractions across shared cell faces and omits sub-cell interface area, so this numerical closure is not a validated physical interface model.

## Single-branch W energy-accounting pilot

The one-branch pilot uses Lin W `Ce/G`, a 100 fs normalized temporal Gaussian with the same volumetric energy in the electron subsystem, and Tolias's solid-W lattice heat-capacity fit. It is a 0D energy-ledger check; spatial conduction, `ke`, melting, and the Ag/W interface do not enter this test.

- Initial state: `Te=Tl=500 K`; input energy density `1.7162e+06 J/m³`.
- Peak electron temperature: `518.426 K`.
- Time for half of the input energy to appear in the lattice: `0.417 ps`; 90%: `0.965 ps`.
- Final lattice energy at 20 ps: `1.6725e+06 J/m³` (`97.451%` of input; the remaining energy stays in the electron reservoir at equilibrium).
- Maximum absolute total-energy ledger residual: `6.25e-11` of input; threshold `1e-08`; **PASS**.

The two initial-state timescales are both reported. At 300 K, `Ce/G=0.1939 ps` and `τeq=0.1908 ps`; at 500 K, `Ce/G=0.3019 ps` and `τeq=0.2942 ps`. Here `τeq=Ce Cl/[G(Ce+Cl)]` is the constant-coefficient two-reservoir temperature-difference time; `Cl` comes from Tolias's solid-W `Cp(T)` and density fits, with `Cp` used as a narrow-range `Cv` proxy.

## Status and limits

`AgW_TTM_baseline_v1` is selected as the repository's exploratory material-parameter baseline. The W energy accounting passes. The independent W low-temperature experimental `G0` consistency check does not pass for the Lin digitized value; this mismatch remains attached to the baseline. Ag `G` is digitized and graph-based at low temperature. The corrected Ag conductivity closure now has a reasonable 300 K anchor, while its high-Te nonequilibrium shape remains exploratory and explicitly dependent on the inferred `A/B` ratio.

The Ag/W electronic interface is numerically closed by the provisional face rule above, but its conductance and sub-cell area approximation are not physically validated. The reused-core Ag/W grid smoke check status is **PASS**; it exercises a synthetic grid, not atoms or LAMMPS. The gated Ag/W LAMMPS coupling entry point is `models/ag_w_qsgs_200A/ttm/agw_ttm_md_pilot.py`; it remains unrun until a statically accepted Ag–W initial configuration and explicit pulse envelope are available.
