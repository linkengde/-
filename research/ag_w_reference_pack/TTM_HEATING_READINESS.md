# Ag-W TTM heating readiness

## Requested geometry and heating method

The candidate model uses x/y periodic boundaries and a free z surface (`p p f`), with fixed atoms at 0–20 Å, a thermostat layer at 20–40 Å, a Newton layer at 40–200 Å, and empty sputter/evaporation monitor regions at 200–250 Å and 250–300 Å. Laser heating is to be done with TTM; no direct thermostat pulse is substituted.

## Why there is no TTM trajectory yet

The installed LAMMPS build contains TTM styles, but the existing project preflight for `fix ttm/mod` found that its boundary assumptions reject nonperiodic z. Changing z to periodic would make atoms that leave the free surface wrap through the vacuum to the bottom, so that stock fix cannot be used with this geometry as-is.

There is an Ag-only finite-volume TTM reference solver in `models/ag_ti3sic2_qsgs_1to1_finer/ag_ttm_reference.py`. It supports periodic x/y and zero-flux open z faces and has been coupled to short LAMMPS Python API pilots. The Ag–W adaptation is now in `models/ag_w_qsgs_200A/ttm/agw_ttm_reference.py`; it reuses the original CIC mapping, finite-volume diffusion, and lattice kinetic-energy handoff while adding separate Ag/W electron fields and a provisional face-based interface term. A synthetic-grid energy smoke check passes. This remains a Python prototype, not a compiled LAMMPS fix, and the Ag–W LAMMPS runner is gated because no full-size structure has passed static acceptance.

The spatial heat-source formula and candidate values were migrated into an independent Ag–W static preflight. Its 10 Å Ag mask was built from the original, overlapping type-2 atom coordinates; at `S(t)=1`, the mapped Newton-layer Ag power was 9,784.984 eV/ps. This is a geometry-specific on-state power, not a pulse energy, and is **stale for any future reconstructed coordinates**. Do not reuse this power or mask: recalculate after a candidate passes static structural acceptance. The historical report, JSON, and mask/source arrays are in `models/ag_w_qsgs_200A/ttm_source_preflight/`. The legacy Ag–Ti₃SiC₂ files were not changed.

The Ag–W pack now has public XTANT-3 Ag and W `Ce(Te)`, `G(Te)`, and `ke(Te)` tables in `ttm_data/XTANT3_TTM_parameters_v3/`; original values and units are retained. The user-uploaded README and license notices are also archived beside the tables. The standalone `Ce` files are from dynamical simulations at the gamma point, while the `Ce` columns inside K tables are from ideal crystals with a 7×7×7 k-point mesh; these definitions are not interchangeable. At sampled common temperatures, XTANT W `G` is 3.8–17.9 times lower than the Lin et al. digitized curve. The source Zenodo record warns that room-temperature values may not always be reliable. See `ttm_data/XTANT3_AG_W_DATA_AUDIT_20261124.md`, `ttm_data/XTANT3_AG_W_SOURCE_AUDIT.json`, and `literature_20261123/README.md` for the table audit and provenance.

`ttm_data/AgW_TTM_baseline_v1/` records the selected exploratory baseline: Lin 2008 `Ce/G` for both Ag and W, Daraszewicz 2014's W `ke(Te,Tl)` Drude closure, and a room-anchored Lin Drude-form Ag `ke(Te,Tl)`. The raw XTANT Ag K table's `16.56 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹` at 300 K is retained for comparison but not selected as the room-temperature magnitude; the new Ag closure gives `429 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹` at `(Te,Tl)=(300,300) K`. Its high-Te `A/B` ratio is inferred from the XTANT 300 K scattering-channel split and is exploratory. The W low-temperature Lin `G` check is outside Daraszewicz's measured 1σ `G₀` interval; the digitized 300 K Lin point is itself extrapolated, and the baseline keeps this discrepancy visible without rescaling the Lin curve. One Lin-W 0D energy-ledger pilot passes its numerical conservation threshold. The earlier W `Ce/G` source audit remains in `ttm_data/W_EPH_TIMESCALE_AUDIT_20261124.md`; it is a literature audit, not a request to run three TTM trajectories.

The selected baseline is explicit, but it is a practical exploratory closure rather than a fully measured set of `(Te,Tl)` surfaces. Ag conductivity now has a reasonable room-temperature anchor; its high-Te shape remains model-based. W conductivity uses the Daraszewicz Drude expression. Daraszewicz's low-temperature W `G0` anchor does not agree with the Lin digitized 300 K extrapolation within its quoted 1σ interval. The Ag–W grid prototype now has a provisional face-based `h_e=1 GW m⁻² K⁻¹` interface closure, using Au/Pt and Au/Al method analogues; it is not an Ag/W measurement and omits sub-cell interface area. The spatial source profile is recorded, but `S(t)` has no target pulse width or temporal envelope, and no Ag–W-specific absorbed fluence is set.

The inherited spatial Gaussian profile is preserved. For a first TTM-MD pilot, explicitly choose `S(t)`, pulse duration, and input energy; confirm how the 20–40 Å Langevin layer should act during heating/cooling; and record the provisional face-based Ag/W interface rule. Lattice heat transport is handled by the atomistic EFS dynamics in this coupled path, rather than a separate continuum `k_l` term. A pure continuum TTM calculation would still need lattice-conductivity inputs. The selected baseline and its single-branch W energy ledger are documented in `ttm_data/AgW_TTM_baseline_v1/`; no three-branch 0D trajectory comparison is needed for this baseline decision. The new literature collection includes Ag/W phase and melt references, but the 1988 phase diagram is calculated, not experimentally measured.

Full-size spatial TTM and atomistic TTM-MD are `NOT_RUN`; the single W 0D energy-ledger pilot and a synthetic two-phase grid-only Ag/W energy smoke check have run. The gated LAMMPS driver is prepared but has not been run. V3 local slabs are mechanically relaxed, but the v3 full-size candidate failed the static-start audit after 10,000 EFS minimization steps, with collapsed contacts and atoms above the material surface. The original perfect-lattice assignment still has a 0.08 Å Ag–W distance and must not be integrated. The new coarse-QSGS v4 candidate preserves the box and near-1:1 mass ratio but its initial interphase contacts have not passed static EFS relaxation. No 300 K atomistic equilibration or sourced LAMMPS TTM-MD has started.

## TTM acceptance inputs

Before a small laser-heating pilot, record:

1. Use the recorded AgW TTM baseline v1 tables and functions with their stated ranges and provenance. Keep the W low-temperature `G0` mismatch visible; do not silently rescale the Lin curve. No source averaging is used.
2. The Ag–W Python reference bridge in `models/ag_w_qsgs_200A/ttm/`, which supports `p p f`, Dai EFS `eam/fs`, Ag/W type distinction, separate phase grids, and equal-and-opposite electron/lattice energy exchange. Before atomistic use, the exact data file must pass the static audit gate and the LAMMPS coupling pilot must be checked against its energy ledger.
3. Temporal envelope `S(t)` or absorbed fluence/total deposited energy, pulse duration, confirmation that the candidate lateral radius/depth are appropriate for Ag–W, and whether the 20–40 Å layer is a heat sink during the pulse.
4. The current code uses a provisional `1 GW m⁻² K⁻¹` face conductance and equal-and-opposite phase-electron transfer; vary and disclose this assumption because it is based on Au/Pt and Au/Al analogues, not Ag/W. Check source deposition and equal-and-opposite electron-lattice transfer before interpreting a trajectory.

No full-size 3D structure has passed static acceptance. The original and v1/v2 candidates have not been equilibrated at 300 K, heated, or used for production MD. V3's small local slabs were zero-temperature EFS minimizations only.
