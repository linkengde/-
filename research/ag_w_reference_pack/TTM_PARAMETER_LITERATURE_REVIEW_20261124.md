# Ag–W TTM literature and data-source review

Date: 2026-11-24  
Scope: identify what the supplied papers and XTANT-3 tables support for an exploratory laser-driven Ag–W melt/cool/resolidification model. This is a source audit; it does not silently turn plotted or derived values into a validated parameter table.

## W electron–phonon time-scale comparison

The source-separated calculation in `ttm_data/W_EPH_TIMESCALE_AUDIT_20261124.md` reports `tau ≈ Ce(Te)/G(Te)` in ps for the native Lin-digitized and XTANT-3 branches, and a limited low-temperature comparison using Lin-method `Ce` with Daraszewicz's experimental or ab-initio `G₀`. The full pointwise table and reproducible generator are `ttm_data/W_eph_timescale_comparison.csv`, `ttm_data/W_eph_timescale_summary.json`, and `scripts/compare_w_eph_timescales.py`.

On the exact shared 500–19,000 K grid, XTANT/Lin `Ce/G` is compared point by point without interpolation; the ratio range and corresponding temperatures are recorded in the JSON summary and generated report. The CSV also carries the separate `Ce` and `G` ratios. `Ce/G` is only a fixed-lattice characteristic estimate, not the exact eigen-time of a coupled two-temperature system. The Daraszewicz `G₀` values are shown only at 300, 500, 600, and 1,000 K and are not extended into a high-electronic-temperature curve. These parameter branches remain separate sensitivity cases; no averaging is justified by this comparison.

## Selected baseline v1

The repository baseline is recorded in `ttm_data/AgW_TTM_baseline_v1/`. It uses Lin 2008 Ag and W `Ce/G` curves, W `ke(Te,Tl)` from Daraszewicz's equation (6), and a room-anchored Lin Drude-form Ag conductivity. The Ag `ke(300,300)` is 429 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹; its high-Te shape uses an `A/B` scattering ratio inferred from the 300 K XTANT channel split and remains exploratory. The raw XTANT Ag K table gives only 16.56 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹ at 300 K because its electron-phonon channel is 16.66 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹; it is retained as source data but not as the selected room-temperature magnitude. The W Lin `G(300 K)=2.124×10¹⁷ W m⁻³ K⁻¹` digitization is 51.7% above Daraszewicz's measured central `G₀=1.4(3)×10¹⁷` and outside the quoted 1σ interval; the Lin point is also an extrapolation below 600 K. The baseline records this discrepancy and does not rescale Lin `G` to conceal it. One single-branch W 0D energy-ledger pilot passes; no XTANT/Daraszewicz parallel 0D trajectory suite was run. This is not a melting or 3D Ag–W interface validation.

Even with these sources, the full-size atomic structure remains the immediate blocker: the retained-atom QSGS candidate failed its EFS static-start check. No 300 K atomistic MD, spatial TTM, or TTM-MD dynamics were run; only the standalone W 0D energy-ledger pilot was run. The exact geometry and force results are documented in `models/ag_w_qsgs_200A/interface_reconstruction_v3/qsgs_candidate/FULL_SIZE_STATIC_AUDIT.md`.

The candidate W table is generated reproducibly by `scripts/digitize_lin2008_w_ttm.py`; the data are in `ttm_data/W_TTM_Lin2008_digitized_300_20000K.csv` and the pixel calibration/source hash are in `ttm_data/W_TTM_Lin2008_digitization_report.json`. The `G` curve below 600 K includes the explicitly stated low-temperature extrapolation. The user-supplied XTANT tables, hashes, and cross-source comparison are in `ttm_data/XTANT3_TTM_parameters_v3/` and `ttm_data/XTANT3_AG_W_DATA_AUDIT_20261124.md`. These remain candidate sensitivity inputs, not a validated Ag-W TTM model.

Additional phase/interface papers are archived: the 1988 Ag-W phase diagram is a calculated model with no experimentally determined phase diagram data; Torre et al. report W-Ag segregation/demixing under their studied processing conditions; Waseda and Ohtani report liquid Ag structure; and Li et al. report Au/Pt and Au/Al electronic conductance, not Ag/W conductance. None of these closes an Ag/W phase diagram or Ag/W electronic interface term.
