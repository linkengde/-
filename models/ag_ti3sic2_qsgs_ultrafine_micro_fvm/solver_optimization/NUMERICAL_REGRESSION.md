# NUMERICAL_REGRESSION

## Solver equivalence checks

The regression suite checks fused Numba enthalpy/capacity/conductivity against the original material functions, Numba face conductance and stencil/residual against the reference, the instrumented baseline step against the original `advance`, and a Numba implicit step against the reference. It also checks conservative EW energy closure, checkpoint round-trip equality, and exclusion of Ti-only cells from the maximum Ag liquid diagnostic.

All six regression tests pass. Random mixed-material property fields agreed to relative error below 2×10⁻¹⁴; face/residual checks are below 3×10⁻¹⁴. On the small mixed-cell step, reference and optimized temperature fields matched within the test tolerance and energy accounting remained below 10⁻¹⁸ J.

The existing analytical/program benchmark driver was also rerun without retaining its generated files in the historical `phase_change_validation/results/` directory. All eight checks passed in 9.27 s: enthalpy round-trip, adiabatic melt, cooling/freezing, Stefan melt, Stefan freeze, interface-resistance steady conduction, uniform 300 K hold, and source-off boundary energy balance. The melt and freeze Stefan front errors were each about 0.01753%; the 100³→200³ space change was 0.02505%, the last tested time-step change was 0.00309%, and the Stefan energy metric was 6.21×10⁻⁹. Fresh results are preserved under `results/RERUN_*`.

## 50³ thermal-cycle comparison

With the same seed1105 QGS map, candidate material parameters, source field, 1 ns heating, 2 μs cooling, `dt-factor=0.25`, and 120 accepted steps, reference fixed-PCG and Numba `ew_safe` endpoints agreed:

- Heated-endpoint Ag bulk liquid fraction: 3.784598071747104×10⁻⁴ (reference) vs 3.784598080298608×10⁻⁴ (Numba), relative difference about 2.26×10⁻⁹.
- Normalized energy residual: 9.51×10⁻¹⁰ vs 9.98×10⁻¹⁰.
- Maximum Ag liquid fraction reached 1.0 in Ag-bearing cells; final cooled liquid fraction was 0.
- Numba required 6,217 PCG iterations vs 16,291 in the reference; solver-step time was 15.98 s vs 62.91 s.

The separate 50³ preheated-state AMG probe reached about 10.01% Ag-weighted bulk liquid fraction in one numerical heating step. FFT and AMG had a 4.62×10⁻¹¹ K maximum-temperature difference and 4.48×10⁻¹² relative difference in bulk liquid fraction. This broad-liquid state is a solver stress probe only, not a validated physical melt prediction.

## 100³ and 150³ limits

The complete 100³ safe-policy cycle closed the energy balance to 1.43×10⁻⁹ normalized residual. The 150³ short stage closed it to 2.24×10⁻¹⁰. Neither run has a mesh-convergence result or a material-source audit upgrade. The source remains the existing numerical localized volumetric pulse; it is not an arc boundary.

No latent heat, Ag melt point, mushy-zone width, or model tolerance was changed to obtain acceleration. The 1 K numerical regularization is retained. The earlier analytical Stefan and time-step convergence values remain in `phase_change_validation/`; those cases were not regenerated by this stage's six kernel/restart tests.
