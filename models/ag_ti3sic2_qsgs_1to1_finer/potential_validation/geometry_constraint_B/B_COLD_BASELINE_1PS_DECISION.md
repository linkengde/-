# Model B: final bounded cold-baseline decision

Date: 2026-10-25. The approved sequence called for one 0.25 ps no-source diagnostic, then a single finite attempt to address the initial-state problem if that diagnostic failed. Both tests are now complete. No heat source or TTM was used in either test.

## Runs and measurements

### 0.25 ps no-source NVE diagnostic

The mobile-Ag temperature fell from 300.00 K to 227.29 K. Total potential energy rose by `1681.836 eV`, kinetic energy fell by `1681.778 eV`, and total-energy drift was only `0.058 eV`, consistent with the separately measured NVE integration drift. Maximum mobile-Ag force increased from `1.452` to `4.685 eV/Å`. The starting-to-ending mobile-Ag RMS displacement was `0.286 Å`; the 1275-atom geometric source core had RMS displacement `0.380 Å`. This did not pass the cold-state gate.

### One 1 ps NVT baseline preparation attempt

This was one bounded run with the same fixed scaffold, WCA geometry obstacle, starting Ag coordinates, 300 K initialization, and 0.5 fs step. The thermostat brought Ag from 300 K to 317.3 K at the end, but the structure did not settle: maximum Ag force rose from `1.452` to `7.453 eV/Å`; total potential energy decreased by about `848 eV` overall and by about `988 eV` during the final 0.25 ps. Mobile-Ag RMS displacement over the 1 ps window was `0.849 Å` (p90 `1.319 Å`, p99 `3.132 Å`); in the source core it was `1.198 Å` (p90 `2.008 Å`).

The pure-Ag provisional CNA-plus-displacement flag marked 14.46% of mobile Ag and 34.04% of the source-core Ag, but the largest connected flagged clusters were only 349 and 71 atoms, respectively. This flag is not calibrated for the composite. CNA unknown fraction was already 71.06% initially and reached 72.35% globally and 93.18% in the source core; LAMMPS also warned that roughly 63,600 atoms had too many CNA neighbors. These measurements cannot be interpreted as a stable liquid pool.

## Decision

**The current Model B preparation fails the cold baseline gate after the one permitted bounded preparation attempt.** Do not start the 1 ps TTM heating pilot, and do not continue another FIRE/NVT relaxation cycle on this same starting state. The current B trajectory would mix substantial structural rearrangement with any heating response, so it cannot support a melting or resolidification interpretation.

This is a no-go for heating this exact B initial state. To resume the local-melt objective, a new initial-state preparation/model definition is needed; any new attempt must have its own finite acceptance criteria. The current geometry-only WCA model remains exploratory and does not provide physical Ag–Ti₃SiC₂ interface binding or a mobile ceramic response.

## Reproduction and archive

Run the finite NVT attempt from the model directory:

```bash
OMP_NUM_THREADS=4 /workspace/.venvs/lammps/bin/lmp -sf omp \
  -in potential_validation/geometry_constraint_B/in.ag_bounded_nvt_1ps \
  -log potential_validation/geometry_constraint_B/log.ag_bounded_nvt_1ps
```

`cold_nvt_1ps_metrics.json` is generated by `analyze_b_cold_diagnostic.py` from the archived start/end snapshots. The run input, LAMMPS log, snapshots, and metrics are retained alongside the earlier 0.25 ps NVE gate.
