# Raw-FCC Ag initial-state screen for geometry-constrained Model B

Date: 2026-10-25. This is one bounded, no-source check of the Ag coordinates directly generated by the QSGS builder, before the archived Ag-only FIRE-100 preparation. It is a separate starting configuration, not another relaxation of the FIRE-100 state.

## Setup

- Geometry: the relaxed-parent QSGS structure, 916,534 atoms, with the rigid Ti/Si/C scaffold and the same WCA-only Ag-obstacle terms used by Model B.
- Ag: 179,246 Newton-layer Ag atoms initialized at 300 K; lower 20 Å and ceramic held fixed.
- Dynamics: NVE, 0.5 fs, 100 steps (0.05 ps); no source, TTM, or thermostat.
- Reproduction from the finer-model directory:

```bash
OMP_NUM_THREADS=4 /workspace/.venvs/lammps/bin/lmp -sf omp \
  -in potential_validation/geometry_constraint_B/in.raw_fcc_cold_screen_0p05ps \
  -log potential_validation/geometry_constraint_B/log.raw_fcc_cold_screen_0p05ps
python potential_validation/geometry_constraint_B/analyze_raw_fcc_cold_screen.py
```

The analyzer reads the compressed start/end dumps archived beside this report.

## Results

| Metric | Step 0 | 0.05 ps | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Ag kinetic temperature | 300.00 K | 237.91 K | −62.09 K |
| Potential energy | −535,459.17 eV | −534,020.50 eV | +1,438.67 eV |
| Kinetic energy | 6,950.77 eV | 5,512.12 eV | −1,438.65 eV |
| Total energy | −528,508.40 eV | −528,508.38 eV | +0.02 eV |
| Maximum mobile-Ag force | 2.135 eV/Å | 2.606 eV/Å | increased |

Mobile-Ag displacement over the screen was 0.123 Å RMS, 0.179 Å at p90, 0.252 Å at p99, and 0.454 Å maximum. Energy conservation is good, but the potential-to-kinetic conversion and cooling show that the generated coordinates are still undergoing substantial structural adjustment. The increase in maximum force is consistent with that interpretation.

## Decision

The raw generated FCC coordinates also fail the cold baseline gate. This rules out the simple alternative of skipping FIRE-100 and heating the raw geometry. It does not indicate an integrator instability: total-energy drift is only 0.02 eV over this short screen. No heating was performed.

Do not run a TTM heating trial on either the raw-FCC or FIRE-100 whole-layer state. The next finite B variant is a localized ROI test: keep the same rigid geometry-only scaffold, but freeze Ag outside a bounded neighborhood of the source so distant Ag rearrangement cannot dominate the local cold baseline. This is an explicitly constrained exploratory model and would need its own cold-state gate before any heating.
