## Microstructure preparation before generating a micron geometry

The target micron-scale Ag-channel and Ti₃SiC₂-ligament dimensions are not present in the checked model tree. Do not choose a micron voxel pitch or QSGS correlation length until an experimental source or agreed morphology specification supplies them. A fair pair keeps 70:30 volume fraction, porosity convention, specimen dimensions, and loading fixed; it changes the measured internal correlation/feature scale and uses multiple independent realizations at each scale.

For each realization, compute the same descriptor set: phase fraction and porosity; `Sᵥ` with a voxel-aware estimator; directional chord-length distributions; connected components and periodic winding; phase-specific connectivity; local throat/neck distribution with a resolution qualification; anisotropy; and two-point correlation length. Increase RVE size and realization count until these descriptors and effective conductivity/capacity estimates plateau within the ensemble spread. A repeated tile is a finite-size check for one realization, not a statistical RVE.

The current nano box is only about 30×30×16 nm. A strict same-physical-size model that resolves nanometer throats can become prohibitively large as the macroscopic specimen grows. Estimate memory/runtime from `N_cells ≈ (Lₓ/Δx)(Lᵧ/Δy)(L_z/Δz)` using measured target scales before generating or launching a full micro/nano pair. For an explicit 3D finite-volume solve, a first-order work estimate at fixed physical time scales roughly as `N_steps × N_cells ∝ Δx⁻² × Δx⁻³ = Δx⁻⁵`, before parallel and interface-conductance effects. Thus if the measured feature scale differs by a factor of 10³ while cells-per-feature and outer dimensions stay fixed, the illustrative explicit-work ratio can reach 10¹⁵. This is a scaling warning, not a project wall-time prediction; the actual micro feature, sample dimensions, boundary conditions, and time step are not yet specified, so a wall-time estimate would be fabricated. Benchmark the new solver on a representative small patch after those inputs are known. If direct resolution is not feasible, use measured-statistics RVEs to derive scale-specific effective transport and a local resolved submodel under matched macroscopic boundary histories; do not present homogenized fields as resolved melt-pool topology.
